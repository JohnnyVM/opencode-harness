# Architecture Package v2 decision record

For each significant D-ID decision, keep one compact record in Proposed Design:

- **Decision:** chosen design and scope.
- **Evidence:** repository paths, specification clauses, or cited sources.
- **Alternatives:** structurally meaningful options considered.
- **Trade-offs:** benefits, costs, and constraints of the choice.
- **Consequences:** interfaces, responsibilities, migration, and verification.
- **Evidence basis:** verified fact, inference, or assumption.

Do not repeat the complete record in the summary, comparison, and ticket plan.
Link those sections to the D-ID and state only the context they need.
