# Arena: candidates and synthesis

Build at least two candidates that differ structurally in boundaries,
ownership, flow, or placement of responsibilities—not merely names. Ensure
each satisfies the frozen specification or identify its precise trade-off.

Compare candidates against relevant explicit criteria: requirement fit,
repository fit, interface simplicity, change locality, failure behavior,
testability, compatibility, operational cost, and risk. Do not force every
criterion where irrelevant. Recommend a candidate or a clearly described
synthesis, explain what was retained or rejected, and state consequences and
remaining uncertainty. Do not implement code.
