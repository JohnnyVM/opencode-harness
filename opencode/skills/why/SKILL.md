# Why: evidence and rationale

Every consequential claim should have a traceable basis: frozen specification
clause, repository path/symbol, test behavior, or cited external source. Label
inference and assumption. Explain why the selected design fits better than
alternatives and what it costs. Do not turn absence of evidence into evidence
of absence.
